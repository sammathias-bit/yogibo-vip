How it works

How the program works.

From your invitation to your first post, this is everything in order.

Yogibo VIP is a hand-selected group of creators who already live in their space and show it. There is no application and nothing to buy. You receive a code with a credit on it, choose what you want from the line, and we ship it to you free. In return, you create for the campaign in your brief and tag @yogibobags.

    Yogibo VIP Creator Agreement

    1. The gift

    Yogibo will ship the products you selected at no cost to you. The gift is yours to keep whether or not the campaign continues.

    2. Content and brief

    You agree to create and publish the deliverables listed on this page, following the creative brief in your invitation, within the window stated there and counted from confirmed delivery. The brief sets the campaign direction; the style is yours.

    3. Disclosure

    You will disclose the gift in each post as required by the FTC Endorsement Guides and your platform's branded-content rules.

    4. Usage rights

    You grant Yogibo a non-exclusive, worldwide licence for 12 months from each post's publish date to repost, embed and promote that content, with credit to your handle. You retain ownership.

    5. Term and ending it

    Either side can end this agreement in writing. If you have received product but not yet posted, we ask that you complete the deliverables or return the product.

    6. Nothing else

    This is the entire agreement. No exclusivity, no non-compete, no minimum follower requirement, no penalties.
